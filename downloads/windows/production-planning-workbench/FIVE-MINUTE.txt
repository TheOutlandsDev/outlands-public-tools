FIVE-MINUTE FICTIONAL PLANNING EXERCISE

1 | Open Planning Demo.cmd
The new private board starts at 160 remaining demand, 25 assigned dock stock,
85 allocated WIP and 50 uncovered units. There are 3 proposed lots.
Stage-7 dock stock is assigned explicitly and cannot also count as WIP.
Review the two product tables and shipment-readiness declaration limits.

2 | Export the baseline and record a review
Choose Export private report. The status gives a reports/<id> directory
relative to your private instance. It contains plan.json, orders.csv,
allocations.csv, readiness.csv and manifest.json. The manifest binds all four
data files to the revision; it is not a signature or publication permission.
Set Reviewer alias to sample_reviewer and click the first ACKNOWLEDGE button.
The saved review is local only. It cannot authorize production or shipment.

3 | Save a changed scenario
Open Project editor. Near the start of order_history, COPY the entire first
order object and APPEND it after the other order objects (before the final ]
of order_history), including the needed separating comma. In the appended
copy only, change row_version from 1 to 2 and ordered_quantity from 70 to 80.
Keep the original row, order_id, source_revision_sha256 and other fields.
This is an invented caller declaration; the all-1 hash is fictional.
Then change the first handoffs object's order_version from 1 to 2 so its
declared stock assignment refers to the new order version.
Choose Validate and save revision. A malformed or stale save retains drafts.
The board becomes 170 = 25 + 85 + 60, now 4 proposed lots. Revision 2 gets
new review actions, all OPEN; the prior review is retained with revision 1.

4 | Export and inspect history
Choose Export private report again. It creates a new five-file report, leaving
the baseline files untouched. Revision history shows two SHA-256 revisions
with a parent link. Choose Back up database to create a checked SQLite copy.
The backup omits inbox files and previous reports; keep the entire stopped
instance when preserving an operational scenario. No automatic restore.

5 | Stop and reopen
Choose Stop local UI. Reopen Planning Demo.cmd. Revision 2 and its plan remain.
Unsaved editor drafts do not. The app never resets or reseeds an existing
instance. Retain both reports to compare the two invented scenarios.

Alternative: Planning Exercise.cmd performs this revision workflow with
independent numerical, hash, SQLite integrity and stale-write refusal checks.
It creates a separate instance, so your browser scenario is unchanged.
This guide is approximate. Use fictional inputs; no manufacturing authority,
employer records, actual lot reservation or real shipment execution is tested.
