Five-minute fictional manufacturing review

Start: Extract All, then open Manufacturing Review Demo.cmd.
Wait for the two exercises to finish and the actual local report to open.

1 / Capacity
Three individually eligible proposals can still exceed combined capacity.
Compare peak 3 against capacity 2 with the revised peak of 2. A missing finish
withholds duration totals; the complete declaration accounts for 9 active +
3 held = 12 ticks. A one-piece flow disagreement remains beside its correction.

2 / Materials
Four kits require four motors and eight filters. Two motor labels refer to
one four-piece canonical lot. Claims of 2 + 3 overreserve it by one. Filters
are separately one short. Inspect both the failed and corrected proposals.

3 / Inspection
Compare 3 of 4 inspection records with complete coverage. Four records can
still contain two outside outcomes. A separately selected later reading leaves
three within and one outside. These are arbitrary teaching values.

4 / Outcomes
Read 4 = 2 first-pass good + 1 reworked good + 1 scrap. First-pass is 1/2,
eventual good is 3/4 and scrap is 1/4. The pending variant keeps denominator 4.
An invalid history or contradictory disposition withholds results.

5 / Evidence
Expand the receipt tables. Open one input and its report. Compare a file's
SHA-256 with the receipt or RUN-MANIFEST.json. Keep the original diagnostics.
Close the report and use --open to verify/reopen the saved run without replay.

Expected: 36 checks / 17 expected review outcomes / operator review REQUIRED.
This demonstrates declared-byte consistency, not physical manufacturing or
authority to operate, accept equipment or publish operational records.
