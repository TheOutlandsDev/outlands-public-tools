This bundle includes CPython 3.14.10, the official Windows x64 embeddable
distribution from https://www.python.org/ftp/python/3.14.10/ .
Its license and third-party notices are retained verbatim in runtime/LICENSE.txt.
All runtime binaries retain their original upstream bytes. python314._pth is
configured for the isolated local runtime and the explicit sitecustomize adapter.

The twelve original OUTLANDS tools retain their MIT licenses, origins, validation
notes and exact public source bytes in application/tools/<tool>/.
The two original exercise runners and guides come from the public Toolkit 0.9.0
release, packaging/source commit 5ace0cacafe0874fa4db816a11c5cc0cf04089d2.
The portable launcher, results renderer and import adapter are MIT-licensed
original OUTLANDS source developed with AI assistance. They do not change the
original tools or grant rights to publish private operational data.
