# Authorized security assessment record

- Engagement identifier and authorization reference, retained privately:
- Included assets, permitted tests and explicit exclusions:
- Approved time window and stop conditions:
- Lab/target baseline and evidence of required isolation:
- Submission/build identity and tool versions:
- Finding: observed behavior, reproduction, impact and uncertainty:
- Remediation and replacement-build identity:
- Retest: performed procedure, actual result and remaining exposure:
- Reporting destination, handling restrictions and disclosure agreement:

Use fictional submissions and findings in public examples. Keep real targets, credentials, submissions, findings and evidence outside the public toolkit. Label containment designs and unrun procedures as proposed. An assessment manager's recordkeeping does not itself enforce target isolation or authorize testing.

The [Offline Assessment Record Contract Checker](../tools/assessment-record-checker/README.md)
can check a separate neutral ledger's declared links and workflow states. Its
consistent result does not verify this record, testing, authorization or fixes.
