# Fictional example and demo themes

For new examples, demo styling and example filenames in this repository, use only
invented HVAC, AI or game-development references. HVAC remains the default for
manufacturing/service flows. AI pipeline/evaluation concepts or game-development
simulations may illustrate a flow when useful. Keep tool names, source filenames
and input contracts generic; this convention is scoped to this repository.
Do not introduce references to private workplaces, customers or operational systems.

Use invented HVAC equipment and service scenarios as the default theme for future
manufacturing/service tools and examples. Useful concepts include air-handler kits,
blower modules, motors, panel filters, component lots, parts shelves, service-kit
benches and declared handoff receipts. Keep the algorithms and input contracts
generic so other domains can supply their own private declarations.

Use new symbolic IDs, quantities and ordinal events. Do not copy real customers,
job sites, equipment serials, product catalogs, addresses, drawings, wiring diagrams,
service records, credentials, deployment details or private business policies.
Fictional arithmetic is not an equipment design, sizing, compatibility or maintenance
instruction. Do not imply that any invented model or receipt represents a real unit,
customer, approved installation or completed service visit.

This convention applies to new examples and deliberate future revisions. Existing
published examples and pinned release snapshots keep their recorded provenance.
The first four-tool HVAC cohort is documented in
[Material & Lot Integrity](BATCH-69-MODULAR-MATERIAL-LOT.md).
