member. Invalid references/protocols withhold qualified results. Valid policy/state
findings retain complete diagnostics. Unknown outcomes and missing history never
become retry-ready; equal claims are not counted as real executions.

Unknown-outcome acceptance blocks retry readiness and leaves ledger claims unresolved.
