description: Create a resumable project handoff when the user asks to pause, continue later, or move to a fresh session.
---

Read .kiro/devkit/handoff-template.md. Save a concise handoff to .kiro-kit/handoff.md covering the objective, current branch/state, changed files, exact successful and failed commands, relevant decisions, remaining work, and authorization limits. Keep facts tied to evidence and distinguish planned work from completed work. Record model/provider only from client evidence. Exclude credentials and unrelated personal history. At resume, recheck drift before relying on old results.
