Failed and unfinished jobs retain partial outputs. An unfinished job blocks new writes. Stop the server, assert every instance process is stopped, then use CLI recover --confirm-stopped. Recovery marks interrupted jobs; it neither resumes nor deletes them. A physical completion marker without a completed journal record is not adopted automatically.


Advisory retention review
Latest snapshots, holds, failed verification, unfinished jobs and selected restore-proof rules protect records. A candidate is advisory only; this app offers no deletion or cleanup command. Retention does not reclaim space.
