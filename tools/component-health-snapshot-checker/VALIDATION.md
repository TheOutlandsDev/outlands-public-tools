# Validation and interpretation limits

The health-incident-checks workflow qualifies Python 3.12/3.13 on Windows and Ubuntu.
Native hardlink, linked-parent, changed-input, fresh-file and overwrite fixtures are
required. Only opposite-platform methods may skip; local volume skips are not native proof.

Known-answer/adversarial tests cover inclusive age/zero-age/future/missing observations,
all nonhealthy states, exact snapshots and ambiguous observations; ordinal chronology,
same-time order, reopen/resolution, missing/future-only histories and invalid future
transitions; anchored inclusive alert windows, non-sliding bridges, independent keys/
components, deterministic grouping, distinct same-content alerts and full accounting;
missing checks/receipts, failed/unknown outcomes, stale/future evidence, policy and
incident pins, role normalization and prevention of best/latest receipt selection.
Every identity list checks duplicate/conflicting/rejected-row accounting and withholding.
Pinned public JSON/I/O helper tests preserve source-tree, overwrite and fixed-error guards.

Actual standalone acceptance copies each complete directory independently, verifies
source pins, runs examples, writes fresh reports, refuses overwrite/source output,
checks raw-input pins/fixed errors and verifies unchanged inputs/no action artifacts.
A connected invented HVAC exercise reviews fresh health declarations, a resolved
incident history and complete alert groups, then explicitly adapts those separate
reports into declared recovery-check evidence. A fictional restore-drill receipt is
only a declaration; no drill runs. Stale health, a reopened incident and an old drill
receipt block readiness. A separate game-service metadata fixture contains no assets.

These synthetic checks do not establish actual service health, authentic incident
histories, real alert correlation, complete dependencies, genuine recovery evidence,
successful drills, privacy, rights, production suitability or recovery authorization.
Original private implementations, records, logs, policies, configurations, paths,
credentials, topology and operating details were not consulted or selected.
