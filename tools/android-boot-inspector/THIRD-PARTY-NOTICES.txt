Boot / Vendor Boot Header Inspector

The selected parser, report integration, tests, guides and fictional generator
are original MIT-licensed code. First-party collection image-reading and
inspection-support mechanisms are reused under MIT; preserve the supplied
LICENSE when redistributing them.

Python and its standard/bundled libraries are supplied separately under their
applicable terms. Qualification downloads hash-pinned CPython distributions into
new owned prefixes; those archives are excluded from this source ZIP and are not
relicensed under this project's MIT grant.

Android/AOSP documentation and format declarations were consulted for binary
format facts. Their original rights and notices remain with their owners at the
primary references linked in docs/PROVENANCE.md. No AOSP implementation, header
source, native utility, parser binary or fixture image is copied or vendored.

Authored fixtures are generated locally and are deliberately non-bootable. No
payload extraction, decompression, signature/AVB verification or device result is
claimed. Inspected images and derived reports retain their applicable rights.
