Primary format background is documented in docs/PROVENANCE.md. This bounded
literal metadata grammar does not evaluate SELinux policy, effective access,
ownership databases, capability application or deployment/security correctness.

rights; hashes do not authenticate origin, source rights or safety to deploy.
