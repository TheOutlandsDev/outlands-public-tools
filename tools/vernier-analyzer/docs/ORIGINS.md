The owner authorized publication of the selected first-party Rev 3b implementation.
Four original files retain exact bytes in `original/`; BASELINE.json pins each.
The two implementation names are duplicates, not two independent engines.
The original check script and revision notes are unchanged. No numerical test suite
was present in that selected patch directory; the 17 release tests are separately
authored and distinguish legacy behavior from the new CLI policies.

The adapter, synthetic generator, tests, source manifest, dependency locks, packaging
and guides are new supporting files. The original algorithm, QC fallback, 10 um pitch
assumption and pixel-scale behavior are preserved. The original on-drive directory
is never edited. Unrelated revisions, images, screenshots, XML, measurement results,
customer/workplace material, archives and downloaded runtimes remain unselected.
No whole-family or whole-drive publication clearance is implied.

packages retain their own licenses; see THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md and DEPENDENCIES.json.
