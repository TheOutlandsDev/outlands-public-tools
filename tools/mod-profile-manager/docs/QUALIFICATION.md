## Storage, privacy and boundary limits

Activation writes only the selected managed workspace and retains previous trees.
Known metadata replacement and native no-replace tree commits do not establish a
general transaction, rollback, power-loss durability or atomic hostile-writer
snapshot. Operations require trusted local ancestors and quiescent data. Races,
filesystem support, I/O errors and partial stages/metadata remain material limits.
There is no purge/automatic cleanup or profile replacement command. History IDs
sort lexically, not by time. Retention consumes space and is not an independent
backup. Unsupported primitives fail closed rather than falling back to replace.

Supported byte/layout copying does not preserve or scrub full ownership, ACLs,
timestamps, alternate streams or external metadata. Names/paths/hashes/IDs and
GUI fields can identify content. No anonymization, encryption, game compatibility,
effective rights or security verdict is established. Public validation keeps
sanitized counts, versions and byte identities rather than raw logs, operational
workspaces, actual paths, model/game content or real profile names.
