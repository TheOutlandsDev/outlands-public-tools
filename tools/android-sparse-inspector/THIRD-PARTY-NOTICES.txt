Android Sparse Image Inspector: original MIT source, tests, documentation and
fictional fixture generator. Includes first-party MIT file/report infrastructure
and bounded-reader design from the outlands-public-tools collection.

Python and its standard/bundled libraries are supplied separately under their
applicable terms. Qualification downloads hash-pinned CPython distributions into
new owned prefixes; those archives are excluded from this source ZIP and are not
relicensed under this project's MIT grant.

The official AOSP sparse-format header and sparse reader were consulted as
format references. They retain their own copyright and Apache-2.0 notices at
the upstream locations linked in docs/PROVENANCE.md. No upstream implementation,
converter, binary or fixture is copied, vendored or relicensed here.

Authored example files are generated locally and are not usable firmware or a
reconstruction set. No payload CRC, reconstruction or device result is claimed.
Inspected inputs and reports derived from them retain their applicable rights.
