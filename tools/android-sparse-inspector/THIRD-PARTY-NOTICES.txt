Android Sparse Image Inspector: original MIT source, tests, documentation and
fictional fixture generator. Includes first-party MIT file/report infrastructure
and bounded-reader design from the outlands-public-tools collection.

Python is installed separately under its own terms. No runtime, AOSP binary,
upstream parser implementation, firmware or vendor fixture is bundled.

The Android Open Source Project sparse format and reader were consulted as
format references. They retain their own copyright and Apache-2.0 notices at
their upstream locations; no source implementation is copied or relicensed here.
Inspected inputs retain their own applicable rights.
